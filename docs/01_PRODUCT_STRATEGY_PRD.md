# LeadSprint AI — Product Strategy and PRD

## Promise

> LeadSprint contacts every new property enquiry, qualifies the buyer in their
> preferred language, and gives sales the context needed to act immediately.

## First customer

- Hyderabad residential developer or broker team
- 5–30 salespeople
- 300+ Meta leads per month
- Telugu, Hindi, and English buyers
- Slow or inconsistent manual response today
- Owner or sales head can approve a managed pilot

## Pain

Real-estate teams pay for leads but lose intent through slow response, repeated
manual qualification, poor follow-up, and no reliable handoff record.

## V1 scope

Include:

- Manual workspace onboarding
- Meta Lead Ads connection and deduplication
- Consent/suppression/quiet-hour policy
- One Bolna qualification agent
- Structured qualification and explainable score
- Sales handoff with assignment and SLA
- Approved WhatsApp follow-up
- Site-visit booking and Google Calendar event
- Today, Leads, Calls, Handoff, and Connections screens
- Audit trail, usage records, monitoring, and kill switches

Exclude:

- Property inventory management
- Generic CRM and marketing automation
- Multiple voice providers
- Self-serve onboarding
- Advanced analytics and Copilot
- Multi-region or microservice architecture

Hide excluded modules in the pilot rather than presenting simulations as live.

## Core users

Owner/sales head:

- Sees response speed, hot leads, site visits, failures, and cost per outcome.

Salesperson:

- Understands a qualified lead in under 30 seconds, accepts ownership, calls,
  and books a visit.

Lead:

- Receives a timely, respectful call and clear follow-up with an easy human path.

## Success metrics

- Median lead-to-first-call time
- Contact and qualification rate
- Hot-lead handoff time
- Site-visit booking rate
- Cost per booked site visit
- Duplicate/lost action rate
- Salesperson correction rate

## Paid-pilot acceptance

- One real Meta lead completes the full workflow.
- Every external action is visible and auditable.
- No cross-tenant access or duplicate provider action is possible in proof tests.
- Every failure has a recovery path and accountable owner.
- Remaining limitations can be explained honestly to the customer.
