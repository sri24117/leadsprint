# LeadSprint AI — Launch and Go-To-Market

## Goal

Win 3 paying design partners by selling one outcome:

> Contact property enquiries while intent is fresh and send sales only the
> buyers worth immediate attention.

## First segment

- Hyderabad residential developers and broker teams
- 300+ monthly paid leads
- Clear project/campaign and sales owner
- No mandatory CRM replacement
- Willing to run one managed campaign pilot

Avoid large enterprises requiring procurement, custom CRM replacement, multiple
telephony providers, or autonomous unsupervised calling in the first pilot.

## Managed pilot

- One workspace and one project/campaign
- Controlled monthly lead volume
- One Bolna agent and approved scripts
- Meta Lead Ads, WhatsApp, and optional Google Calendar connection
- Human-supervised hot-lead handoff
- Weekly outcome and transcript-quality review
- Setup fee, monthly platform fee, and provider usage priced transparently

Treat pricing as a discovery hypothesis until customer interviews and unit costs
validate it.

## Discovery

Ask about monthly leads, response time, contact rate, languages, qualification
questions, site-visit conversion, sales assignment, DND/consent process, existing
tools, and the value of one additional booked site visit.

## Pilot metrics

- Lead-to-call time
- Contact and qualification rates
- Handoff acceptance time
- Site visits booked and attended
- Cost per booked visit
- Duplicate/lost actions
- Support hours per workspace
- Gross margin after provider usage

## Expansion gate

Do not expand product surface until the first workflow is retained, onboarding
is repeatable, support is manageable, provider costs are understood, and at least
several customers can explain the value in outcome terms.
