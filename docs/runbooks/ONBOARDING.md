# Runbook — Customer Onboarding

1. Confirm staging/production health, monitoring, backup, worker, and proof tests.
2. Create the workspace, admin, timezone, quiet hours, concurrency, and spend cap.
3. Record the customer's consent/DND process and escalation owner.
4. Connect Meta and submit a test lead; verify signature, hydration, and dedupe.
5. Connect Bolna, configure approved qualification script, and make a staff test call.
6. Connect WhatsApp and verify approved template delivery plus opt-out handling.
7. Optionally connect Google Calendar and create a test site visit.
8. Run the complete golden path and inspect every audit/job/provider record.
9. Train sales on Handoff, SLA, correction, booking, and failure escalation.
10. Schedule weekly outcome and transcript-quality reviews for the pilot.

Never activate live calling until policy gates, kill switch, balance/spend limit,
and a monitored staff test have been confirmed.
