# LeadSprint Positioning

## One-Line Positioning

LeadSprint is a Telugu/Hindi AI caller for real estate teams that contacts every new lead instantly, qualifies budget/location/timeline, books site visits, and sends WhatsApp summaries to sales.

## Who It Is For

- Hyderabad and Telangana real estate builders
- Broker teams running Meta or Google lead ads
- Project sales teams with slow lead response
- Agencies losing leads due to missed calls or weak follow-up

## Core Pain

Real estate teams pay for leads, but speed-to-lead is poor. Leads go cold when sales teams call late, miss calls, or forget follow-up.

## Hybrid Product Position

LeadSprint is not a generic voice AI platform. It is a real estate lead conversion product with a hybrid voice layer:

- Vapi for fast premium pilots.
- Bolna for self-hosted/high-volume control.
- Mock mode for local demos and reliable sales walkthroughs.

This gives you a better story than "we built another AI caller": you can promise speed today and cost/control later.

## Promise

Every lead gets called within seconds. The sales team receives only structured, qualified next actions.

## Pilot Offer

7-day pilot on 50-100 leads:

- Instant AI call
- Telugu/Hindi/English qualification
- Budget, location, timeline capture
- Site visit booking
- WhatsApp summary
- Dashboard report

## Sales Script

Sir, you are already paying Meta and Google for leads. The leakage is speed-to-lead. LeadSprint calls every lead instantly in Telugu/Hindi, qualifies budget and location, then sends your team a WhatsApp summary. Can I run it on 50 leads for one week and show you how many extra site visits we recover?

## Proof Metrics

- Lead response time
- Contacted rate
- Qualified rate
- Site visits booked
- Human follow-ups needed
- Missed calls recovered
